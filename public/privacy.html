Privacy policy

Updated 9 November 2026

Who is responsible

Sympal is made by Ahmet Bugra Avcilar, who decides what happens to the data on this page and answers for it

Write to abavcilar@gmail.com about anything here

What is kept

When you join the TestFlight waitlist: your email address, the time you joined, and the words of the box you ticked, as a record of what you agreed to

Nothing else: no name, no IP address, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers

Why

To send you an invite to the Sympal beta on TestFlight, and for nothing else

No newsletter, no marketing, and your address is never sold or handed to anyone for their own use

On what basis

Your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, given by ticking the box, and yours to take back whenever you like

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and the database the list is kept in · Like any web host, it sees your IP address to serve the page, but the waitlist does not keep it
  • Apple, when the beta opens: you either get a public TestFlight link by email, or your address is added as a tester in App Store Connect and Apple sends the invite
  • Google, if the invite is written by hand: it goes from a Gmail account

These companies may process data outside the EU, under the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework

How long

Until the TestFlight beta ends, and then the list is deleted · Sooner if you ask

Your rights

You can ask to see what is kept about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to take back your consent · Write to abavcilar@gmail.com and it is done within a month, usually much sooner

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live
